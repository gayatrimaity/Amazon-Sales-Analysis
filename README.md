# Amazon-Sales-Analysis
# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis | Power BI Project

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard** built using Power BI. The goal is to analyze sales performance, product trends, and customer engagement using key business metrics.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The dashboard helps answer key business questions:

* What are the overall sales trends over time?
* Which product categories generate the most revenue?
* Which products perform best in terms of sales and customer engagement?
* How do sales vary across months and weeks?

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset includes:

* Order Date
* Product Category
* Product Description
* Sales (Revenue)
* Quantity Sold
* Customer Reviews

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Key KPIs

* **YTD Sales:** $2.18M
* **QTD Sales:** $811.09K
* **YTD Products Sold:** 27.75K
* **YTD Reviews:** 19.42M

### Visualizations

* Sales by Month (Trend Analysis)
* Sales by Week (Granular Trends)
* Sales by Product Category (Performance Breakdown)
* Top 5 Products by Sales
* Top 5 Products by Reviews
* Interactive Filters (Category, Quarter)

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Sales show a **steady upward trend**, with stronger performance in later months.
* A few product categories contribute **significantly higher revenue**, indicating concentration.
* Top-performing products drive a large share of total sales.
* Products with high reviews often correlate with higher demand.
* Weekly trends reveal **fluctuations and peak periods**, useful for operational planning.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Focus on **high-performing product categories** to maximize revenue.
* Promote top-reviewed products to leverage customer trust.
* Investigate low-performing categories for improvement or discontinuation.
* Use seasonal trends to optimize inventory and marketing campaigns.
* Align promotions with peak sales periods identified in weekly trends.
